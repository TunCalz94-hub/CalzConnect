OUR STORY

Every great business begins with an idea.

Calz Connect began with a simple belief: people should have better ways to discover products, build meaningful business relationships and access opportunities.

The idea was developed by a university student in Uganda who saw an opportunity to create something meaningful despite having limited resources. Instead of waiting for perfect conditions, the founder chose to start with an idea, determination and a vision for the future.

Inspired by the power of entrepreneurship, networking and the importance of putting customers first, the concept developed into a business built around connecting people with products and opportunities.

Calz Connect's planned founding date is 1 January 2027, in Uganda. Its ambition is to grow into a trusted platform where customers and affiliates can connect, participate and create value together.
